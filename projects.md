  <h3>RampDB 2: Predicted DNA Methylation Database</h3>
  <p class="entry-meta">Lead Developer · Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health · July 2024 – May 2025</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Started building a public, interactive database of predicted DNA methylation (DNAm) to support research on epigenomic mechanisms and therapy.</li>
    <li>Used PCA and UMAP to analyze bulk, single-cell, and spatial RNA-seq data and reconstruct the DNA methylation landscape.</li>
    <li>Built the user-facing interface as an R Shiny app so researchers can explore the data and gene regulation.</li>
  </ul>
